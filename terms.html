Terms of use

Digital Familiar Backups is a tool for the operator's personal Digital Familiar installation.

Intended use

The operator runs the integration on their own equipment and authorizes a Google Drive account they control. This site provides information about that installation. It does not offer public account registration, hosted backup storage, or a commercial backup service.

Only authorize this application if you operate the associated Digital Familiar installation and understand its requested Drive permission.

Operator responsibilities

The operator controls the backup destination, protects application credentials and recovery keys, maintains adequate storage, monitors backup status, and performs recovery rehearsals. The operator should only back up data they are entitled to process and store.

Successful upload verification confirms that the downloaded encrypted bytes match the uploaded encrypted archive. A recovery rehearsal separately checks whether that archive can be decrypted and restored. Backups and restores can fail, and this tool provides no guarantee of uninterrupted availability or recovery from every loss.

Privacy and third-party services

The privacy policy describes Google data access, encryption, retention, and removal. Google Drive, GitHub, and any other providers used by the operator remain subject to their own applicable terms and storage limits.

These information pages do not charge a fee. The operator controls any separate provider subscriptions or purchases.

Stopping use

The operator may stop the integration and revoke its Google authorization at any time. Stored backups remain until separately removed. Keep any recovery key needed for retained encrypted copies.

Contact

Use the user support email listed on this application's Google authorization screen to contact the Google Cloud project owner about this installation.
